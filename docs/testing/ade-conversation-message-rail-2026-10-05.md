## Orchestra change and verification

The new [`MessageRail.tsx`](../../apps/desktop/src/features/workspace/chat/MessageRail.tsx) is available after three user prompts in panes at least 512 pixels wide. Its narrow left-edge hit area reveals the rail on hover or keyboard focus. The compact ticks preview a prompt and its last assistant reply and scroll to the prompt; selecting text in the preview does not trigger a jump. The conversation composer no longer renders the expandable token usage and session details panels. This is a presentation change; native session data remains available to the client for its actual runtime controls.

Acceptance scenarios: three prompts show three jump targets; hover shows the paired reply; selecting a target scrolls the chat; a narrow pane has no rail; the removed panels are absent.
